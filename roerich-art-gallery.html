About Roerich Art Gallery

The Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery at Naggar preserves paintings and memories associated with Russian artist Nicholas Roerich.

History

Roerich settled in the Kullu Valley in the early 20th century. The estate and gallery celebrate his Himalayan paintings, artistic work and connection with the mountain landscape.
