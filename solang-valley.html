About Solang Valley

Solang Valley is a spectacular mountain destination known for snow activities, paragliding and panoramic views.

History

Traditionally used as a grazing valley, Solang developed into one of Himachal Pradesh's best-known adventure destinations. Seasonal activities make it popular throughout the year.
