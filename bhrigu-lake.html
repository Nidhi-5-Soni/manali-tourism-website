About Bhrigu Lake

Bhrigu Lake is a high-altitude alpine lake surrounded by meadows and Himalayan peaks.

History

The lake is associated with Sage Bhrigu in local tradition. The surrounding meadows and trekking route have made the area an important destination for nature lovers and trekkers.
