About Vashisht Temple

Vashisht Temple is a traditional stone and wood temple in the village of Vashisht, famous for its hot springs.

History

The temple is dedicated to Sage Vashisht and is believed by local tradition to be ancient. The village has long attracted pilgrims and travelers seeking the natural hot springs and peaceful mountain setting.
