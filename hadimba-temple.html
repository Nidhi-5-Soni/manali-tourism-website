About Hadimba Temple

Hadimba Temple is a remarkable wooden temple dedicated to Hidimba Devi, set among tall cedar trees near Old Manali.

History

Built in 1553, the temple is associated with the Mahabharata and the local legend of Hidimba, the wife of Bhima. Its pagoda-style wooden architecture makes it one of Manali's most recognizable cultural landmarks.
