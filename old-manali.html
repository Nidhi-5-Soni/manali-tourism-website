About Old Manali

Old Manali combines traditional village character, apple orchards, cafés and views of the surrounding mountains.

History

The old settlement preserves elements of traditional Himalayan village life. Its narrow lanes, wooden houses and proximity to Manu Temple make it a favorite area for cultural exploration.
