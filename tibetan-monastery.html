About Tibetan Monastery

Manali's Tibetan Monastery is a colorful center of Tibetan Buddhist culture, prayer and handicrafts.

History

The monastery represents the Tibetan community's cultural presence in Manali. Visitors can observe prayer wheels, traditional artwork and a peaceful atmosphere in the heart of town.
