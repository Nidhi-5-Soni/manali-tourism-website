About Mall Road

Manali Mall Road is the lively commercial center of the town, filled with shops, cafés, restaurants and local crafts.

History

As tourism grew in the Kullu Valley, the central market became an important meeting and shopping area. Today it is one of the busiest pedestrian-friendly parts of Manali.
