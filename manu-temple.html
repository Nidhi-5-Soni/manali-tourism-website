About Manu Temple

Manu Temple in Old Manali is dedicated to Sage Manu and is surrounded by dramatic Himalayan scenery.

History

Local tradition connects the site with Manu, the legendary lawgiver and progenitor of humanity. The present temple is an important pilgrimage site and a popular stop while exploring Old Manali.
